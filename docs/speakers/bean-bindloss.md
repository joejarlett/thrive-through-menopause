Bean Bindloss
Bean Bindloss is a registered Nutritional Therapist and Integrative Hypnotist with a special interest in helping women navigate the often confusing transition of perimenopause, as well as menstrual and hormonal health challenges such as PMOS, PMDD and endometriosis.


She combines evidence-based nutrition with a compassionate, whole-person approach to uncover what's really driving symptoms such as weight changes, fatigue, brain fog, digestive discomfort and intimate health concerns such as Genitourinary Syndrome of Menopause. Recognising that health is shaped by physical, mental, emotional and spiritual factors, her work supports the whole person, not just the symptoms. Rather than offering one-size-fits-all advice, Bean helps women understand their unique physiology and guides them through practical, sustainable changes that support long-term health and wellbeing.
Based in Bruton, Somerset, Bean works with women both in person and online through 1:1 consultations and group programmes.


Talk
Never finding the right word. Forgetting why you walked into a room. Feeling bone tired. Watching the weight pile on despite doing all the things that used to work. For many women, these confounding and infuriating changes can seem to arrive out of nowhere during perimenopause.


In this talk, Bean explores what's actually happening beneath the surface, and how it impacts your memory, your energy, sleep and weight. Discover how shifts in oestrogen impact the way your body and brain use energy, why metabolic flexibility becomes increasingly important in midlife, and practical ways to support your energy, cognition and metabolic health through the perimenopausal transition.
