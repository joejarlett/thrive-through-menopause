Dr Elizabeth Thompson 


CEO and Integrative Medicine Doctor, Podcaster, National Centre for Integrative Medicine (NCIM), UK 


Dr Thompson is passionate about supporting a transformation of healthcare towards an empowered Integrative Medicine Model that combines conventional, holistic, functional and lifestyle approaches to support health and wellbeing. She trained in Medicine at the
 University of Oxford and in Palliative Medicine in the West of Scotland to consultant level. She also trained in a range of integrative approaches including Mistletoe Therapy, and now leads a busy Mistletoe service within NCIM that also includes expertise in medicinal mushrooms, nutrition and fasting during cancer treatments.  


In 2014, she founded the National Centre for Integrative Medicine (NCIM), a not-for-profit social enterprise, focusing on clinical, education and community as well as a membership service connecting those passionate about advancing an Integrative Healthcare model that prioritises whole-person wellbeing.  


Part of the movement for change in healthcare includes a focus on education, which led Dr Thompson to set up an accredited 2-3 year master's level 7 Diploma in Integrative Healthcare, focusing on clinical, business and research elements to support healthcare professionals into a career in Integrative Medicine. More recently, Dr Thompson has launched
 a vibrant and cutting edge podcast Integrative Medicine Matters in 2025 along with NCIM Academy accredited CPD short courses in Integrative Healthcare
