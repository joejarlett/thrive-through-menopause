About the session:




Root and rise 


A Movement Practice for Women in Midlife & Beyond




Private/Workplace Classes




Entering midlife and the menopause transition doesn’t have to mean you lose your energy, clarity, or confidence. In fact, with the right kind of lifestyle tweaks, you can feel more powerful than ever. I promise you….




I’ve designed a new practice that anyone can do (particularly those of you who haven’t exercised for a while) that will leave you feeling a greater sense of vitality but also a deep sense of calm and contentment.




Root and Rise blends dynamic Chinese Exercises with the transformative energy of Qigong — a 4,000-year-old practice that restores your balance, clears emotional tension, and recharges your life force.




The first half of the class is designed to gently increase your heart rate and get your muscles working - but not so much that it stresses your body or makes you want to walk out the door! Unless we have eaten good levels of protein and exercised regularly
 we may have lost up to 20% of our muscle mass by the time we hit 50. So we need a different kind of class that strengthens our bones and keeps us fit and toned but we can’t expect our bodies to work out like they might have been able to in our twenties! Not
 straight away anyway...




The second half of the class is all about getting in touch with your body, your energy, how you’re feeling and quieting your mind. Honestly it’s the perfect combination.

About Me: 


At 47 perimenopause knocked me for six. Amongst many things, I began to have unexplained epileptic seizures. As the MD of a multi million pound company and a single parent, being suboptimal just didn’t seem like an option. I felt a huge pressure to continue with 'business as normal' but actually what I needed to do was tune into the way my body was changing and work with it - to minimise the physical challenges and really embrace the opportunity to create a life that nourished me in every way. 


My training as a Nutritionist, Yoga and Qigong teacher as well as 30 years in senior management positions meant that I had the tools I needed to experiment on myself - to go on a personal health journey,  and then having talked to dozens of other women, create a programme that drew on all of our experiences. 




This programme became   The Natural Menopause Method. The Root and Rise practice is a core component of that method.
