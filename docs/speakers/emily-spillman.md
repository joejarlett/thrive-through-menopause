About me


Emily Spillman is a Pelvic Health & Continence Nurse, Mummy MOT practitioner, scar therapist and women's health and strength coach, and the founder of Holora Health, a multidisciplinary pelvic health and wellbeing service based in South Gloucestershire. With a clinical background spanning nursing, long term conditions management, cancer rehabilitation and pelvic health, Emily takes a whole woman approach, recognising that hormones, movement, nutrition, rest, mental health and connection all shape how we feel, and that the pelvic floor sits right at the centre of it all. At 37, Emily has been navigating perimenopause for most of her thirties, having inherited the early start that her mum and grandmother also experienced, and that lived experience has deeply shaped the way she cares for women, supporting them holistically without overwhelming them or skipping the basics. She's also the founder of Fannies and Flapjacks, a relaxed, evidence based women's health event that she delivers in cafes and workplaces, proving you can talk about leaky bladders and hot flushes in the same breath as cake. Emily believes no woman should be told that symptoms in midlife are simply something to put up with.


-What I'm bringing to the day


I want to have open, honest conversations with women about their pelvic floor and vulval and vaginal health, from bladder leaks and prolapse to dryness and discomfort, because the more openly we talk about the parts of menopause we've been taught to keep quiet, the more confident women feel in seeking the right support and moving through this stage of life feeling strong, informed and in charge of their own health.
