Meet Sheetal Jeswal


Sheetal Jeswal is a Menopause Champion, community campaigner, broadcaster and founder of South Asian Voices Bristol CIC, bringing a personal and community perspective to the conversation around perimenopause and menopause.


Currently experiencing perimenopause herself, Sheetal speaks openly about her own journey — from the emotional and physical changes to the impact it can have on confidence, relationships, work and everyday life. She is passionate about creating spaces where women, particularly South Asian women, can talk honestly about experiences that have too often been misunderstood, overlooked or kept private.


Through South Asian Voices Bristol CIC, Sheetal has developed campaigns and community initiatives to raise awareness of menopause and women's health, including powerful public-facing billboard campaigns designed to put South Asian women and their experiences at the heart of the conversation.


At this event, Sheetal will share her own experiences of navigating perimenopause, alongside insights from her community work and campaigning. She'll explore why representation matters, the importance of breaking the silence around menopause, and how we can create more open and supportive conversations for women across different cultures and communities.


Come and hear Sheetal's honest, personal and empowering perspective on perimenopause — and why no woman should feel that she has to navigate it alone.
