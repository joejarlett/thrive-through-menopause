Ruth Bradbrook BIo 


I am a Yoga Teacher, Soul Midwife, Sacred Space Holder, and Facilitator.
I serve as a bridge between heaven and earth, guiding others on the sacred path from the seen to the unseen - a wayfinder for those navigating life’s thresholds and soul awakenings.
Rooted in ritual and presence, I create containers for transformation, deep rest, and spiritual reconnection - spaces where you can pause, breathe, and remember your wholeness.
Yoga offers not only strength and flexibility but mental clarity, emotional balance, energetic connection, and spiritual insight.


For me, yoga is a way of life: a mirror between time on the mat and the ever-changing flow of daily life. It’s where I meet myself with honesty, softness, and love.
Each practice brings me home again, offering wisdom that supports me as a woman, a soul midwife, and a guide. I am forever a student, honoured to teach from a place of lived experience and reverence.
