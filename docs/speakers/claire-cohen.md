**Claire Cohen**

Bio 08.2026

As a registered naturopathic nutritional therapist, I specialise in nutrition to support women through the metabolic and hormonal changes of the menopause transition.

I believe healthy eating should be enjoyable, achievable and sustainable — not another set of diet rules to follow. Through my clinical practice, talks, group programmes and hands-on cooking workshops, I combine evidence-based nutritional science with practical knowledge to help women understand what their bodies need and, importantly, to feel confident putting that knowledge into practice.

My own experience of perimenopause led to a dramatic career change, and today I feel privileged to help other women navigate this stage of life with greater knowledge, confidence and joy of food.

I believe food is fundamental to health. The nutrients we get from what we eat provide the building blocks our bodies need for energy, repair, and resilience.

My aim is simple: to cut through the confusion and give women the knowledge, skills and confidence to nourish themselves well through menopause — both in the consultation room and in the kitchen.

I am looking forward to presenting a series of interactive, workshops exploring the nutrients found in the foods we eat and how they can support our health and wellbeing. Together, we’ll create simple, delicious, nutrient-dense snacks that are easy to make and designed to help fuel you through the day.
