Caroline Gaskin is a Homeopath and Womens Health Coach and specialises in hormonal balance and managing menopause naturally.

She originally studied Fine Art Textiles at Goldsmiths & ran her own textile print studio.

In the early 90’s she had her children and started to work in PR for natural birth, complementary health and organic lifestyle.

As her children grew, she re-connected with her spiritual path of observing nature & began to study natural medicine.

In 2000 she qualified as a homeopath.

She’s studied flower essences in West Australia and has attained the full Permaculture Design Certificate.

Caroline helps clients to explore transition create a sustainable health plan.
