It’s got me through some very tricky times especially through the last 10 years where I myself have been navigating the peri menopause. I support a lot of ladies through their own journeys within our Circuits community aswell as going through my own experience so you are most definitely not on your own. No journey is the same, we are as unique as a finger print however together we can turn this tricky part of our lives into something quite empowering.
