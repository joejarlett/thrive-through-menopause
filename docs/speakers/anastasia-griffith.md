Anastasia Griffith Bio


Menopause is an opportunity, not a curse. It asks us to shed old skins and pushes us to finally become the women we were always meant to be" believes trauma informed EFT practitioner, energy psychologist and personal success coach, Anastasia, who specialises in freeing midlife women from the physical and psychological symptoms they believe are inevitable.


After burning out of her own successful acting career in Hollywood 12 years ago, Anastasia dedicated herself to understanding the predicament of modern women - a story of perfectionism, people pleasing, overriding their own needs, and an expectation to "be it all" - that led to her own stress induced premature perimenopause.


Founder of The Cortisol Clinic and The Sovereign Success Studio  Anastasia is passionate about helping her clients soothe the root cause of their midlife collision, so they are free to live their next chapter from love, not fear.
