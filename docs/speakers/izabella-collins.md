Izabella is a holistic wellness practitioner, Kundalini yoga teacher, kinesiologist and intuitive energy channel with a deep passion for empowering women to reconnect with the wisdom of their bodies.


Her work supports women through all ages and stages of life, with a particular focus on women’s hormones, womb wisdom and restoring balance naturally. She weaves together bodywork, kinesiology, somatic practices, nervous system regulation, natural remedies, movement, energy healing and intuitive guidance, supporting women to release stored stress, emotional patterns and trauma that may be impacting their hormonal and overall wellbeing.


Through private sessions, women’s retreats and group experiences, Izabella creates spaces for women to feel held, empowered and deeply connected to themselves honouring the cyclical nature, intuition and innate intelligence of the female body.
