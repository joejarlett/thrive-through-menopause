**Shona Hirons
Award-Winning Menopause Coach | Burnout Prevention Speaker | Bestselling Author | Founder of Mindset in Motion**

At 42, my life changed overnight.

After spending 20 years as a litigation lawyer, I was living on stress, long hours and constant pressure. Then a life-changing cycling accident left me with multiple facial fractures, a brain injury and a long road to recovery. Almost immediately afterwards, my periods stopped completely. My doctor believed my body had simply gone into survival mode.

Two years later they returned, with a vengeance.

The bleeding was so severe it controlled my life. The bloating became so extreme that people regularly asked when my baby was due. A scan revealed multiple growths, some the size of grapefruits, and shortly afterwards I was diagnosed with Stage 3 uterine cancer.

Following a total abdominal hysterectomy at just 44, I was handed leaflets about counselling because I could no longer have children. What nobody prepared me for was surgical menopause.

Within weeks, the night sweats were relentless, my confidence disappeared, my waistline expanded despite teaching up to ten spin classes a week, and I no longer recognised the woman looking back at me.

Rather than accepting that this was simply my new normal, I decided to understand why.

I gained advanced qualifications in nutrition and weight management after midlife, became a certified menopause coach and discovered something that transformed my life: there is no one-size-fits-all approach to menopause. We each have to become scientists of our own bodies.

I swapped endless cardio for strength training, learnt how food, movement, sleep and stress influence our hormones, and completely changed the way I looked after myself.

Today I help women stop fighting their bodies and start working with them.

Through my **Ultimate Menopause Makeover** programme, workshops and keynote talks, I've supported thousands of women to understand their symptoms, rebuild their confidence and discover that menopause isn't the beginning of the end, it's the beginning of a powerful new chapter.

Because menopause shouldn't shrink your life.

It should help you rediscover it.
