Hi, I am here to introduce IFS therapy to women who may be going through a period of transition and anybody who would like to heal and gain more self leadership.


Internal Family Systems is about getting in touch with our inner world through both body and mind, from a place of calm, curiosity, called Self. The principle is that our minds are made up of a mosaic of parts, each with its own agenda, fears and memories… like little people.


I am an IFS therapist, working from a studio in Stoke Bishop, Bristol, and online. 


And for this forum, I will have a stand, with books, cards on IFS, and for us to have a chat. 
I will also offer short IFS meditations for you to connect with your inner world and to start drawing a cartography of your own parts.I am always impressed by each person’s inner system and its unique character.


Something to remember: “There are no bad parts.” — Richard Schwartz, founder of IFS.
