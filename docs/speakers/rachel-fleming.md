✨ Bio for a Peri‑ & Menopausal Audience


Session title:“Menopause: A spiritual journey home”


Rachel Fleming is a contemporary witch, intuitive consultant, and guide for women navigating the profound transitions of peri‑menopause and menopause.
At a time when your body, identity and priorities are shifting, Rachel offers a grounded, soulful space to reconnect with your inner wisdom — not as something mystical or abstract, but as a practical, powerful form of guidance.


Women in midlife often find themselves standing at a threshold: old roles loosening, new truths emerging, and a deeper voice within asking to be heard. Rachel’s work meets you exactly here. Through a blend of psychic insight, her own commitment to cyclical living, embodied practice and psychic clarity, she helps you trust what you already sense, refine your intuitive gifts, and make decisions that honour who you are becoming.


Her sessions support women who are:


•⁠  ⁠Feeling the emotional and energetic turbulence of hormonal change
•⁠  ⁠Questioning long‑held patterns, relationships or work identities
•⁠  ⁠Craving a life that feels more aligned, spacious and true
•⁠  ⁠Ready to reclaim their magic, their voice and their authority
•⁠  ⁠Sensing a shift in purpose but unsure how to articulate or act on it




Rachel’s approach is gentle, mischievous, and deeply human — rooted in lived experience, a devotion to nature, spiritual intelligence, and a fierce respect for the wisdom that emerges in midlife. She works with women who refuse to shrink, who are ready to transform, and who know that intuition is not a luxury but a compass.


Whether you’re navigating emotional upheaval, creative rebirth, spiritual awakening or the quiet, seismic rearranging that menopause brings, Rachel helps you move through it with clarity, dignity and power.
