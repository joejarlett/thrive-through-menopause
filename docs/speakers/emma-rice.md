**Emma Rice
Holistic Health & Wellness Coach | Mindfulness & Breathwork Facilitator | Founder of Flourish with Emma**

I'm Emma, founder of Flourish with Emma, and my passion is helping people reconnect with themselves and create greater calm, balance and wellness in everyday life.

With over 15 years of experience working within healthcare, alongside my work the past few years as a Holistic Health & Wellness Coach, mindfulness meditation and breathwork facilitator, I've seen first hand how easily our own wellbeing and wellness can get pushed to the bottom of the list.

My own journey has shaped so much of the work I do today. It's taught me that looking after ourselves isn't about being perfect or changing everything overnight. Often, it starts with creating space to pause, listen to what we need and make small, sustainable changes that feel right for us.

Through Flourish with Emma, I bring together mindfulness, meditation, breathwork, lifestyle and wellness coaching, alongside immersive relaxation experiences. I create welcoming, down to earth spaces where people can slow down, reconnect with themselves and explore, tools they can take into everyday life.

I'm particularly passionate about supporting women through midlife and menopause a stage of life that can bring enormous change, but can also be an opportunity to reconnect with who we are and what we want from the next chapter.

My approach is very much about the whole person. There is no one size fits all versions of wellness and I believe we should feel empowered to explore what supports us as individuals.

My vision is to create a community where wellness | Wellbeing feels accessible, supportive and real. Giving people the space, tools and confidence to reconnect with themselves, find greater balance and truly flourish. 🌿

My areas of work include:

- Holistic health & wellness coaching
- Mindfulness meditation and breathwork
- Immersive relaxation experiences 🎧
- Menopause and midlife wellness
- Lifestyle and sustainable wellness practices
- Group sessions, workshops and 1:1 support
- Wellness resources, guided meditations and eBooks
