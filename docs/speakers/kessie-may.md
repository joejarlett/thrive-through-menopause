Kessie May is a creative entrepreneur, coach and community builder who brings positive energy, practical tools and a little bit of magic to the way we think, work and live.

Kessie’s own journey has taught her just how powerful our internal dialogue can be. After experiencing a destructive environment in her twenties, she spent years learning to recognise the patterns that had become wired into her thinking, and discovering how to consciously create something different. She understands how self-critical and self-destructive thoughts can move through both our brains and our bodies, and how difficult it can be to change those patterns when they have become familiar.

Today, Kessie combines science-backed approaches to behaviour and mindset with spirituality, intuition and energetic alignment to help people create a more positive flow in their lives. Her approach isn’t about pretending everything is wonderful. It’s about learning to notice what is happening, choosing more helpful ways of thinking and creating the conditions for something better.

This is particularly relevant during menopause, when our brains and bodies are recalibrating and many of the things we have relied on for years can suddenly feel different. Kessie’s work offers a hopeful reminder that we can influence the conversations happening inside our own heads, and that changing the way we speak to ourselves can change the way we experience the world.

Session title:
Mindset &amp; Mantras: Change the Conversation in Your Head, Change Your Life

Talk / activation name:
Mindset &amp; Mantras: Stop Talking Yourself Out of the Life You Want

Description:

What if the voice in your head wasn't constantly trying to keep you safe by keeping you small? This playful, practical session explores the stories, beliefs and internal dialogue that shape the choices we make—and how changing the conversation with yourself can change what you believe is possible. Expect practical mindset shifts, powerful mantras, a little bit of magic and plenty of permission to take up more space, trust yourself and create a life that actually feels like yours.

The session covers:

- **The stories we tell ourselves** - "I'm not ready", "I'm too old", "Who am I to do that?"
- **How our internal dialogue shapes our behaviour.** Where we get pleasure from holding ourselves back
- Spotting the difference between intuition and fear
- **Mantras that actually work** rather than vague positive affirmations you don't believe
- Turning a big desire into an embodied action
- **Permission to want more** — money, creativity, love, visibility, adventure, whatever
- A little ritual/activation where everyone creates their own **"I'm allowed to..."** statement

And crucially, Kessie brings her **Formula for Joy &amp; Abundance** into practical magic.

You don't need to become a completely different woman to create a different life. You need to stop negotiating against yourself.
